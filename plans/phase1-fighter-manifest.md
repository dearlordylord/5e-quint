# Phase 1 Fighter Manifest

## Selected Vertical

- **Species:** Orc, Medium, Speed 30 ft.
- **Background:** Soldier.
- **Ability-score method and exact values:** Standard Array assigned by the SRD Fighter recommendation: Str 15, Dex 14, Con 13, Int 8, Wis 10, Cha 12. Soldier background adjustment: +2 Str and +1 Con. Final scores: Str 17, Dex 14, Con 14, Int 8, Wis 10, Cha 12.
- **Alignment representation:** Lawful Good, stored as the two-axis alignment choice `{ morality: "good", order: "lawful" }`. It is structured input on the character draft and Character Sheet for this vertical; SRD 5.2.1 is the provenance for the alignment rule/options. It has no battle runtime projection in phase 1.
- **Languages:** Common, Dwarvish, Goblin.
- **Fighter level-1 choices:** Primary class Fighter; advancement history exactly one Fighter level-1 entry; skill choices Perception and Survival; Fighting Style choice Defense; Weapon Mastery choices Longsword, Spear, Flail.
- **Equipment/loadout:** Use class starting equipment option C and background equipment option B, then buy Chain Mail, Longsword, and Shield from starting coins. Battle loadout is Chain Mail worn, Shield wielded, Longsword wielded one-handed. Remaining coins are not battle-relevant in this vertical.
- **Selected SRD monster/Stat Block:** Goblin Warrior.
- **Battle setup assumptions:** Two combatants, no surprise, adjacent or otherwise in melee reach, Fighter acts first. Battle starts from a finalized Character Sheet and an authored SRD Goblin Warrior Stat Block, then derives battle-ready creature projections from those boundaries. The Fighter discovers `Attack` and `End Turn`, attacks the Goblin Warrior with the one-handed Longsword, supplies target, attack-roll, and damage-result fills, then resolves End Turn.

## Batch Fill Story

1. Start a new character-creation draft. Initial holes include class, background, species, ability-score method, alignment, and languages. The runtime generates the sheet id, so there is no user-filled name/details hole in this phase-1 vertical.
2. Fill class with Fighter. This opens Fighter holes: two Fighter skill choices, Fighting Style choice, Weapon Mastery choices, and class starting-equipment choice.
3. Fill background with Soldier. This opens background ability-score-increase choice, gaming-set tool choice, and background equipment choice. Soldier also grants Athletics, Intimidation, and Savage Attacker.
4. Fill species with Orc. This fixes creature type Humanoid, Medium size, 30 ft. Speed, Darkvision, Adrenaline Rush, and Relentless Endurance.
5. Fill ability-score method with Standard Array and assign Str 15, Dex 14, Con 13, Int 8, Wis 10, Cha 12. Fill Soldier adjustment as +2 Str and +1 Con. The draft now derives Str 17, Dex 14, Con 14, Int 8, Wis 10, Cha 12.
6. Fill languages with Common, Dwarvish, and Goblin.
7. Fill alignment with Lawful Good.
8. Fill Fighter skill choices with Perception and Survival.
9. Fill Fighting Style with Defense.
10. Fill Weapon Mastery choices with Longsword, Spear, and Flail.
11. Fill class equipment with option C and background equipment with option B. Fill purchase/loadout holes with Chain Mail, Longsword, and Shield; mark Chain Mail worn, Shield wielded, and Longsword wielded one-handed.
12. Re-derive holes. If no required creation holes or legality issues remain, finalize to a Character Sheet. The finalized sheet remains a character; battle receives only runtime projections plus references/readers over the authored Units/stat block.

## Battle Story

Battle initialization consumes the finalized Fighter sheet, the decoded combat-relevant Units, and the authored Goblin Warrior Stat Block.

The Fighter battle seed includes combatant identity, current HP 12, current Temporary HP 0, initiative input, action availability, death policy for a player character, and loadout references to `weapon_longsword`, `armor_chain_mail`, `equipment_shield`, and `defense`. Battle init derives max HP, AC 19, attack bonus, and damage expression through one sheet/Unit reader path rather than storing duplicate battle-ready combat facts beside the derivation references.

The Goblin Warrior battle seed includes combatant identity, the `StatBlockRecord` reference from the SRD-only stat-block collection, current HP initialized to the listed HP 10, current Temporary HP 0, initiative fill, and death policy for a monster. AC 15, max HP 10, initiative modifier +2, and stat-block attacks are read from the authored Stat Block boundary, not copied from the old Core monster catalog and not re-authored in a projection record.

Minimum attack flow:

1. Discover acts for the Fighter current actor: `Attack` and `End Turn`.
2. Choose `Attack`. The selected act opens target, attack-roll, and damage-result holes.
3. Fill target with Goblin Warrior.
4. Fill attack roll for the Longsword attack. Fighter attack bonus is +5. A d20 roll of 10 gives total 15, which hits Goblin Warrior AC 15.
5. Fill damage result for one-handed Longsword as 7 Slashing total, representing `1d8 + 3`.
6. Resolve Attack. Fighter spends the action. Goblin Warrior loses Temporary HP first if any exists, then HP. In the default setup it has Temporary HP 0 and HP 10, so it becomes HP 3 and remains alive.
7. Resolve End Turn. The Fighter's turn ends, action availability is no longer relevant for that turn, and initiative advances to the Goblin Warrior.

Death/defeat behavior for the first slice should be initialized even if the golden attack leaves the Goblin alive. If a Fighter attack reduces the Goblin Warrior to 0 HP, the Goblin dies immediately under SRD Monster Death and A12. If later monster damage reduces the Fighter to 0 HP without instant death, the Fighter gains Unconscious and enters the death-save track under SRD character death rules and A12. This does not require a new `ASSUMPTIONS.md` entry.
