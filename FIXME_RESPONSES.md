## Cross-Cutting Temporal Guidance

The important pattern from these FIXMEs is lifetime separation:

- Creation-session state: draft id, fill protocol, hole frontier, submitted choices.
- Durable character sheet facts: class/background/species, advancement, current long-lived build choices, equipment ownership/default loadout, ability scores, hit point maximum.
- Runtime/encounter state: current HP, temporary HP, remaining Hit Dice, reactions, pending choices, active effects, hand occupancy during battle.
- Provenance/audit metadata: source records, optional lineage, import/corpus details.

When a field is read long after character creation, its name should still describe a durable domain fact. Creation words like `selections` and `sourceDraftId` need either a specific audit workflow or removal from the durable sheet boundary.
