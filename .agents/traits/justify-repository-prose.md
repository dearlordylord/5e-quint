---
name: justify-repository-prose
subject: Repository documentation additions and updates.
requirement: Keep prose only when it serves a concrete future reader or
  recurring operation and that usefulness justifies its search and reading
  burden. Put one-off chronology, progress reports, and task evidence in the PR
  or local artifacts unless that history is needed for a future repository
  decision.
purpose: Keep repository search and reading focused on useful, durable information.
