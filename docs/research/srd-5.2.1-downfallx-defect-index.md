# Confirmed issues in pinned Downfallx SRD 5.2.1 Markdown

This index records **known, individually checked findings**, not an exhaustive
audit of [Downfallx `1b4b99dcb786cdd1a2fb26f8acec1551191f1ca4`](https://github.com/downfallx/dnd-5e-srd-markdown/tree/1b4b99dcb786cdd1a2fb26f8acec1551191f1ca4).
The oracle is the checked-in [official SRD 5.2.1 PDF](../../.references/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf);
page numbers below are **physical PDF pages**. “Corrected locally” means the
present [working RAW corpus](../../.references/srd-5.2.1/) has a repair; it does
not mean upstream changed. For overall corpus-quality measurements, see the
[four-way comparison](srd-5.2.1-downfallx-comparison.md).
