# SRD attribution and trademark notices in comparable projects

Checked 2026-09-23. This is a source survey of official SRD terms and public project notices, not legal advice or a determination that a project is compliant.

## Official SRD and trademark boundaries

For redistributed SRD 5.2.1 material, Wizards' [official SRD 5.2.1 PDF](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf) requires the attribution statement printed in its Legal Information section on page 1. That statement identifies the SRD version and Wizards of the Coast LLC, links to the SRD page, and names and links the CC BY 4.0 license. The same section says not to include any other attribution to Wizards or its parent or affiliates. Use the current PDF's prescribed wording as written; a competitor's paraphrase is not a substitute.

The [CC BY 4.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode) requires attribution when licensed material is shared, including supplied creator, copyright, license, warranty-disclaimer, and source notices; it also requires indicating changes and providing the license text or link. It allows a reasonable presentation suited to the medium. Its scope excludes trademark rights and says the license does not imply endorsement. Those terms do not prescribe a separate D&D trademark-ownership disclaimer.

Wizards' [D&D Beyond SRD page](https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd) lists SRD 5.2.1 and explains that SRD 5.1 and 5.2 content can be used under CC BY with proper attribution. The [SRD 5.1 CC PDF](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.1/SRD_CC_v5.1.pdf) has its own version-specific attribution statement and source URL; if a product uses both versions, keep each source credit aligned to its own SRD notice.

Wizards' [Terms](https://company.wizards.com/en/legal/terms) and the D&D Beyond page footer identify D&D marks as trademarks of Wizards of the Coast (the general Terms also say some marks belong to Hasbro). This is ownership information, separate from the SRD credit. The [Fan Content Policy](https://company.wizards.com/en/legal/fancontentpolicy) has rules about trademarks and logos in fan content; D&D Beyond distinguishes that route from publishing rules content under the SRD/Creative Commons. It should not be treated as the SRD attribution text.

These examples document what projects publish, not what Wizards or Creative Commons require. For an SRD 5.2.1 notice, the controlling source in this survey is the official PDF's page 1 text; do not add a trademark line solely because another project does so.
