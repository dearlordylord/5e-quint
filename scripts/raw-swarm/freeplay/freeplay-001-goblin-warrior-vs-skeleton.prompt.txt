Play a D&D 5e (SRD 5.2.1) battle through the "dnd" MCP server. You control
both sides as the player and DM.

The combatants are a Goblin Warrior with initiative 15 and a Skeleton with
initiative 10. Set up the encounter, make plausible table-level choices for
both creatures, and continue until you judge the encounter complete. Then close
the battle session and briefly report what happened.

Learn the available workflow from the MCP server. Do everything through its
tools and do not write files.
