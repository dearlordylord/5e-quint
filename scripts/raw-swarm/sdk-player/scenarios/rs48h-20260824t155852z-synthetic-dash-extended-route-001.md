Use a brightly lit, obstacle-free geometry-derived arena containing a Skeleton and one distant hostile Wolf. Place the Skeleton 50 feet from a designated blocking position through which the Wolf intends to pass on a later turn. Place the Wolf far enough from the Skeleton, the blocking position, and every eligible route between them that the Skeleton cannot enter the Wolf’s reach and the Wolf cannot enter the Skeleton’s reach during the tested movement. The Skeleton has Speed 30 feet and must reach the blocking position during its current turn to establish the intended later confrontation before the Wolf closes the distance. The player must take Dash for the Skeleton and choose a legal route longer than 30 feet but no longer than 60 feet that ends at the blocking position. Resolve the movement and retain the resulting projection of the Skeleton’s remaining movement for that turn. The Skeleton and Wolf are hostile, but no attack, target selection, Opportunity Attack, interrupt, roll, or damage is required. Include no objects, cover, elevation, difficult terrain, occupied-space traversal, Disengage, or mounts.
