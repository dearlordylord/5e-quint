The intended unsupported SDK probe is canonical Large-creature footprint-aware geometry movement and Opportunity Attack participation. Use one unmounted Riding Horse, two Goblin Warriors, one Wolf, and two Skeletons, all using canonical Stat Blocks; no Character Sheets are needed.

Create a geometry-derived, brightly illuminated, open 75-foot-square arena with a level floor and no objects, cover, elevation, difficult terrain, mounts, or transferable items. Place the Riding Horse at the arena center. Place one Goblin Warrior immediately outside the west edge of its occupied footprint and the other immediately outside its east edge. Place the Wolf 20 feet north of the horse. Place the Skeletons 25 feet northwest and 25 feet southeast of it. The horse is allied with both Skeletons and hostile to both Goblin Warriors and the Wolf. The Skeletons are allied with each other and the horse and hostile to both Goblin Warriors and the Wolf. The Goblin Warriors and Wolf are mutually allied and hostile to the horse and both Skeletons.

Mark three nonphysical 10-foot-square objective zones at the north, southwest, and southeast edges. At the start of its first turn, the horse’s controller chooses the southwest or southeast zone. The horse seriously attempts to enter the chosen southern zone before the end of round 2 and then enter the northern zone before the end of round 5. The Skeletons pursue the Goblin Warriors, trying to force at least one to abandon its flanking position around the horse. Both Goblin Warriors initially maintain pressure from opposite edges of the horse’s footprint. After the horse enters the chosen southern zone, the Goblin Warrior nearer the unchosen southern zone seriously attempts to reach that unchosen zone, while the other Goblin Warrior and Wolf pursue the horse toward the north.

Roll initiative normally. Controllers choose legal actions, targets, routes, and reactions. The horse’s initial departure can cross the reach boundaries of differently positioned enemies; it may Dash, Disengage, attack, or risk those exits. When one Goblin Warrior breaks away toward the unchosen zone, its route may leave the horse’s reach, and the horse’s controller decides whether to spend an available Reaction on an Opportunity Attack. Controllers may instead conserve Reactions when later movement is tactically more important. Resolve all placements, footprint adjacency, route clearance, movement distances, and reach entries and exits through geometry using every square occupied by the Large Riding Horse. The horse remains unmounted and independent, with no object-transfer activity.
