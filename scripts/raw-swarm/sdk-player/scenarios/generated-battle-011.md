Two Goblin Warriors pursue one Skeleton through an illuminated stone storage passage. The Goblin Warriors are friendly to each other. Each Goblin Warrior is hostile to the Skeleton, and the Skeleton is hostile to each Goblin Warrior. Use only their canonical stat blocks, with no Character Sheets.

The passage is 60 feet long and 15 feet wide, with open western and eastern exits. Place the Skeleton 20 feet from the eastern exit. Place the nearer Goblin Warrior 25 feet west of the Skeleton and the farther Goblin Warrior 15 feet west of the nearer Goblin Warrior. A fixed iron portcullis crosses the passage between the Skeleton and the nearer Goblin Warrior. It has irregular gaps and an open five-foot-wide passage at its north end. No visibility condition applies beyond the portcullis ruling below.

The Skeleton seriously intends to escape through the eastern exit without abandoning its effort to delay both pursuers. It moves east while using its ranged attack against the nearer legal Goblin Warrior target. If a Goblin Warrior reaches melee range or occupies a position that blocks the Skeleton’s route to the exit, the Skeleton changes to its melee attack against that Goblin Warrior and moves around the blocker when legal movement permits.

The nearer Goblin Warrior seriously intends to stop the Skeleton before it leaves through the eastern exit. It pursues through the north-end opening, attacks at range while closing, and changes to its melee attack as soon as it can engage the Skeleton. The farther Goblin Warrior seriously intends to help contain the Skeleton. It initially makes ranged attacks through the portcullis. After the nearer Goblin Warrior passes through the opening, the farther Goblin Warrior also crosses but seeks a distinct legal position from which it can attack the Skeleton instead of merely following the nearer Goblin Warrior’s path.

For every attack through the fixed portcullis while attacker and target are on opposite sides, the Table authors the exact ruling that the target has Three-Quarters Cover. The portcullis provides no cover when attacker and target are on the same side. Passing through the five-foot-wide north opening requires ordinary movement through that opening. The Cover degree is Table-owned and is not derived by tactical-space.
