Use one Quasit, one Wolf, and one Goblin Warrior, initialized solely from their canonical SRD stat blocks; no Character Sheets are involved. The intended unsupported-capability probe is per-instance GM-authored circumstance Advantage on a saving throw generated by a canonical stat-block action. Every other required choice proceeds through surfaced Battle acts and ordinary geometry-assisted movement.

Place the combatants with the geometry helper in a dimly moonlit, roofless 35-foot-square shrine court. A dry stone basin occupies the center. A five-foot-wide escape threshold lies in the middle of the east wall. The Quasit begins 20 feet west of the Wolf and 30 feet northwest of the Goblin Warrior; the Quasit is 30 feet from the escape threshold. The Wolf begins 10 feet west of that threshold, and the Goblin Warrior begins 10 feet southwest of it. The Quasit is hostile to the Wolf and the Goblin Warrior, each of them is hostile to the Quasit, and the Wolf and Goblin Warrior are allied with one another. The Quasit is trying to cross the eastern threshold. The Wolf is trying to stay close enough to threaten the Quasit’s ground approach, while the Goblin Warrior is trying to preserve a legal attack and block the most direct route to the threshold. Dim moonlight supplies illumination, and geometry resolves the basin as an ordinary obstacle.

The Quasit’s first act must be its canonical Scare action against the Wolf. The saving throw generated by that action is the encounter’s first materially relevant saving throw. The GM grants the Wolf circumstance Advantage on that one exact saving throw. The grant applies to that save only and to no later attack roll, saving throw, ability check, or other test. This single-instance saving-throw Advantage is the intended unsupported-capability probe.

After Scare resolves, use only surfaced Battle acts and their typed targets, damage, movement, interrupts, and End Turn continuations. The Quasit initially moves along the basin’s northern side toward the threshold while using surfaced attacks when they materially help its passage. If the Wolf controls that route or the Goblin Warrior moves to preserve an effective attack from the eastern approach, the Quasit changes tactics by using its canonical movement modes to approach from the basin’s southern side or from above the obstruction, choosing the legal route that most directly advances it toward crossing the threshold. The Wolf and Goblin Warrior may choose legal attacks, targets, and movement routes while seriously pursuing their distinct tasks. The Wolf closes on the Quasit when it can threaten the approach; the Goblin Warrior maintains distance when possible but moves nearer the threshold if the Quasit bypasses the Wolf. If conditions or movement make either assignment ineffective, they exchange pressure and obstruction duties without changing their pairwise relationships. Resolve Scare, its saving throw, attacks, damage, movement, surfaced interrupts, and End Turn continuations normally. Do not grant any later circumstance modifier.
