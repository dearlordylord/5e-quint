In a brightly lit, obstacle-free 50-foot-square geometry-derived arena, place a Wolf at (25, 20), a Skeleton at (20, 20), and a Goblin Warrior at (25, 25). The Wolf is hostile to the Skeleton and to the Goblin Warrior; the Skeleton and Goblin Warrior are indifferent to one another. The two reactors occupy different positions and each begins within its exact 5-foot melee reach of the Wolf. Mark the Wolf’s den entrance at (25, 45). The Wolf carries food for its pack and has the serious objective of reaching the den entrance during its current turn. The Skeleton has the serious objective of stopping living creatures from approaching the den entrance, while the Goblin Warrior has the serious objective of taking the carried food; each preserves its Reaction for an eligible Opportunity Attack. The GM directs all combatants and chooses whether the Wolf takes Disengage before moving or risks the Opportunity Attack options surfaced while departing both hostile reaches. The GM chooses a legal route to the den entrance that exits both initial reaches, does not reenter either reach, and remains within the Wolf’s Speed. The required frontier ends after adjudicating the Disengage choice, movement, and the surfacing of any Opportunity Attack options; no attack roll, damage roll, later attack, or later turn is required.
