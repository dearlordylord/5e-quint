In a brightly illuminated, obstacle-free geometry-derived rectangular arena 40 feet long from north to south and 20 feet wide, place exactly one Wolf on the centerline, exactly one Goblin Warrior 5 feet directly south of it, and exactly one Skeleton 10 feet directly north of it. The Goblin Warrior and Skeleton are allies. Each is hostile to the Wolf, and the Wolf is hostile to each. The Skeleton has a clear, unobstructed legal Shortbow attack against the Wolf, and the 10-foot gap places the Wolf beyond the Skeleton’s melee reach. Nothing else supplies Advantage or Disadvantage.

Initiative must be Goblin Warrior, Skeleton, Wolf. The Wolf’s immediate objective is to close the distance to the Skeleton and prevent further ranged attacks when its turn arrives if it can act. The Skeleton’s immediate objective is to make a Shortbow attack before that distance can close. The Goblin Warrior’s immediate objective is to occupy the Wolf’s attention and enable that specific ranged attack.

Without changing position, the Goblin Warrior must use the supported Help action for the Skeleton’s attack. Let the player choose the Wolf from the typed Help targets, then end the Goblin Warrior’s turn. On the Skeleton’s immediately following turn, leave the legal attack selection and dice to the player, subject to the chosen attack being the Skeleton’s currently legal Shortbow attack against that Wolf. Proceed only far enough to determine whether the cross-turn, target-specific held Advantage is offered to the intended Skeleton against the intended Wolf and consumed by resolution of that attack before the Goblin Warrior acts again.

Do not add movement, cover, difficult terrain, Prone, Dodge, objects, elevation, reactions, more combatants, or any competing Advantage or Disadvantage. Review against the complete admitted catalogue retains only the canonical Goblin Warrior, Skeleton, and Wolf specified here.
