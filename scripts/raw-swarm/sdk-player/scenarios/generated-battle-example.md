Five 4th-level adventurers must carry a living Medium unarmed Commoner through a defended mine and send it away in a one-use lift. Five Skeletons, three Zombies, and two Wolves defend the route. The party must contain one and only one Cleric, at least one Rogue, and at least one Barbarian or Fighter. The Barbarian or Fighter begins with a two-handed melee weapon in hand. Exactly two adventurers must begin with shields equipped, and exactly one adventurer must begin carrying an exposed source of bright light. The players choose legal classes for any remaining positions, species, builds, spells, weapons, and which qualified adventurer carries the light. No more than three adventurers may have darkvision.

Use a 90-by-60-foot grid, columns A–R west to east and rows 1–12 north to south. An impassable wall runs along the boundary between I and J. The wall can be crossed only between I3 and J3 or across the two-square opening between I9 and J9 and between I10 and J10. Intact wall segments provide total cover. Square pillars occupy E2, H5, C8, and F11. A pillar is impassable and provides half cover when it obscures at least half a target. Ore bins occupy K3–L3, O5–P5, and M8–M9. Bin squares are difficult terrain and their outer edges grant half cover, but they can be entered and crossed.

A four-space lift occupies Q10, R10, Q11, and R11, with its entrance through the west edge of Q10. Its operating chain can be reached from P10, P11, Q10, or Q11. Braziers are fixed at the northeast corners of I3 and Q10. The first is reachable from H3, I2, I3, or J3; the second from P9, P10, Q9, or Q10. They occupy no square. The wall brazier begins unlit and the lift brazier begins lit. A lit brazier produces bright light within 20 feet and dim light for the next 20 feet. An adjacent creature with a tinderbox or open flame can ignite an unlit brazier with an action, and an adjacent creature can extinguish a lit brazier with an action. The change occurs when the action resolves. Each brazier has sufficient fuel for the encounter. The rest of the mine is dark unless illuminated by a creature or effect.

The adventurers begin in C6, D6, E6, C7, and D7, with spaces assigned by their players, and the Commoner begins at E7. Skeletons begin at J3, N3, N5, L8, and N8. Zombies begin at H3, J9, and P10. Wolves begin at J10 and O10. The adventurers and Commoner face east. The defenders face west except the Zombie at H3, which faces east. Facing has no mechanical effect. Nobody is surprised. Roll separate initiative for each adventurer and the Commoner, once for all Skeletons, once for all Zombies, and once for both Wolves. Players resolve ties among creatures they control, the GM resolves defender ties, and player-controlled creatures win any remaining tie. Use printed average Hit Points.

Before initiative, the players assign control of the Commoner to one player. A speaking creature can give it one brief instruction without an action on that creature’s turn if the Commoner can hear the instruction. A new instruction immediately supersedes the old one. The Commoner follows a shortest legal route that advances the instruction, with its controlling player choosing the route that creates the fewest currently possible Opportunity Attacks. It Dashes whenever doing so advances it at least one additional square and otherwise Dodges. If it has no operative instruction or cannot legally advance one, it stays in its square and Dodges. It cannot attack, manipulate a brazier, or pull the lift chain.

Each lift square is one occupant space, and each Small or Medium creature uses one space. A lift carrying the Commoner can carry at most three adventurers. Boarding is ordinary movement through the west edge of Q10. A creature in a square that can reach the chain may pull it with an action. On resolution, all occupants leave the map and initiative immediately. Reactions triggered before the action resolves occur normally, but departure itself neither creates a reaction trigger nor permits damage to interrupt it. The lift is consumed even if sent away empty or without the Commoner and never returns.

The defenders begin with an aggressive southern screen. On its first turn, the Zombie at H3 enters I3 if that square is empty and then holds the northern opening. The Zombie at J9 holds the southern opening. The Wolf at J10 moves through the opening toward the closest enemy it can reach without Dashing, preferring the exposed-light carrier on a tie. The Wolf at O10 stays within 10 feet of P10 until the Commoner crosses the wall. The entrance Zombie holds P10. A Zombie attacks an adjacent Commoner if possible; otherwise it attacks the adjacent creature that most recently moved toward the lift, then the adjacent creature with the lowest current Hit Points.

A Wolf attempts to fight beside another defender so Pack Tactics can apply. It attacks the Commoner if it can reach it, then an adventurer whose movement could reach the lift entrance on that turn, then the exposed-light carrier, then the nearest enemy. It chooses routes that end adjacent to a conscious defender when doing so does not provoke an additional Opportunity Attack. A Wolf reduced to half its printed average Hit Points or less withdraws toward the nearest conscious Zombie, using Disengage if leaving an enemy’s reach and movement otherwise permits it.

Skeletons target the Commoner whenever they have an unobstructed ranged attack. Otherwise they target the exposed-light carrier, a creature adjacent to a Wolf, a creature concentrating on a spell, and then the nearest adventurer, in that order. Lowest current Hit Points, lowest column, and lowest row break ties. A Skeleton without a legal ranged attack moves the least distance necessary to gain one, preferring a bin edge that provides cover and then the greatest separation from the nearest adventurer. Against an adjacent enemy it uses a melee attack and does not voluntarily move through that enemy’s reach.

When the Commoner crosses the wall, the Wolf at O10 advances to intercept it, while the other Wolf stops withdrawing and takes the shortest safe route toward an empty square adjacent to the Commoner. Zombies move toward empty spaces adjacent to the Commoner or P10. Skeletons seek covered positions within 30 feet of Q10. If an adventurer draws both Wolves west of the wall while the Commoner remains there, the Wolves continue pursuing that adventurer only until the Commoner crosses; they then reverse toward the lift. If the Commoner boards, defenders target it when possible and otherwise target creatures able to reach the chain. Once it departs, surviving defenders pursue the closest remaining adventurer and attempt to occupy A6 and A7 before the adventurers reach them.

The adventurers secure victory when the living Commoner leaves in the lift. The defenders secure victory if the Commoner dies or the one-use lift departs without it. These results are final. Continue play afterward only to determine whether adventurers left in the mine defeat the defenders, escape through A6 or A7, or are left with no creature able to act.
