The lane contains no cover, obstacles, objects, or difficult terrain. Geometry-assisted play governs placement, movement, reach, and range. The Riding Horse and Wolf are hostile to each other. The Riding Horse seriously pursues moving west along the lane while using Hooves when attacking the pursuing Wolf is tactically preferable to continued movement. The Wolf seriously pursues overtaking the horse and attacking it with Bite.

The Riding Horse’s player chooses on each turn whether to maximize separation, turn and attack with Hooves, or combine surfaced movement and attack acts when legal. The Wolf’s player chooses its legal route and allocation of movement while pursuing, and uses Bite whenever attacking serves the pursuit.
