Use a brightly lit, obstacle-free 50-foot-square geometry-derived arena. Place a Skeleton at (10,10), a Wolf at (15,10), and a Goblin Warrior at (35,35). Every pair is hostile. The Skeleton must cross toward the northeast corner at (50,50), while the adjacent Wolf must pursue and prevent the Skeleton from opening the distance between them. After each resolved movement by either of those combatants, the Goblin Warrior must intercept whichever one is then closer to the northeast corner. On its first turn, the Skeleton may take Disengage as its Action before selecting a route that exits the Wolf’s reach, or take another surfaced action, including an available attack, before moving away. Disengage suppresses the Wolf’s Opportunity Attack for that movement; departing without Disengage permits the interrupt if its eligibility remains satisfied. The Skeleton’s route is a player choice and can change both its distance from the Wolf and which of them is nearer the northeast corner. Reassess the Goblin Warrior’s target priority from the resulting geometry after the movement. Leave the Skeleton’s action and route, the Wolf’s interrupt decision, the Goblin Warrior’s reprioritized target, and all attacks, rolls, damage, and later movement unresolved.
