In a bright, level 65-by-55-foot arena, two SRD-legal 4th-level adventurers face four Skeletons. Use a square grid of 5-foot spaces; coordinates identify columns from west to east and rows from south to north. The adventurers occupy (2,5) and (2,7), while the Skeletons occupy (11,3), (11,5), (11,7), and (11,9). A 15-foot-wide band of churned sand covers every square in columns 6 through 8 and is difficult terrain without cover or obscurity; every other square is firm and open. Apply the SRD grid rules for entering squares, diagonal movement, and ranges. Each Skeleton has the fixed 13 Hit Points, and Initiative is determined by normal rolls. Before initiative, choose and record both adventurers’ SRD-legal builds, fixed or rolled Hit Points, standard class and background starting equipment, and which listed space each occupies; they receive no additional purchases or magic items. Every combatant starts at full Hit Points with all spell slots, feature uses, Hit Dice, and carried ammunition unspent, and nobody has Temporary Hit Points, a condition, or an ongoing effect. There is no surprise, elevation, outside aid, or objective other than defeating the opposing force under normal combat-ending rules. The Skeletons seriously attempt to win: the outer pair may reposition north and south while using Shortbows, the inner pair may concentrate fire through the central band, and any Skeleton may retreat to preserve distance or switch to a Shortsword when engagement favors melee. The adventurers may advance together through the shortest crossing, separate toward opposite flanks, remain west of the sand and trade ranged attacks, rush one end of the Skeleton line, or change their division of effort as enemies reposition and ammunition, injuries, and expendable abilities matter.
