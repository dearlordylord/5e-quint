Four canonical combatants contest a closed floodgate crank in bright light on stable stone: two Goblin Warriors called Brine and Soot, a Skeleton called Rivet, and a Wolf called Tangle. The crank is not manipulated during these four turns. Brine is hostile to Rivet and intends to knock it prone, reducing its immediate ability to resist pressure around the crank. Rivet is hostile to Soot and intends to grapple Soot, keeping that goblin occupied. Soot is hostile to Tangle and intends to knock the wolf prone before it can disrupt Soot’s position. Tangle is hostile to Brine and Soot and intends to knock prone whichever goblin remains the more effective immediate obstacle when Tangle’s turn begins. Each combatant seriously seeks sole control of the crank’s immediate vicinity for a later attempt to operate it. Use only the canonical Goblin Warrior, Skeleton, and Wolf stat blocks; no Character Sheets are needed.
