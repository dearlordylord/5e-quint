## Notes

Sub-agent originally proposed `ModifyMaxHpOperation`. Landed as EffectAtom (not OngoingOperation) because the spell authors cleanly as an activation/direct phase with a timed duration — matches the Fly/Protection-from-Evil pattern rather than the Bless ongoing_effect shape. The widening is modest: modify_max_hp is distinct from heal_hp, grant_temp_hp, and modify_ac, all of which were previously present.

Also introduced the bare-number fixed-count on choose_up_to. Previously every choose_up_to required a SlotScaling; Aid's "up to three creatures" (3 fixed, no upcast) is the first case where the scaling shape was vestigial. The union `number | SlotScaling<number>` is honest about the distinction.
